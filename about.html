About

Silas Caste is the pen name of Jason Witt.

He began writing under a pen name for personal safety reasons while raising young children. His work draws from lived experience and explores themes of Domestic Violence through means of control, manipulation, and force, and the lasting impact of lives it affects.

His first novel, Interrupted Dreams, is based on true events. The second book in the trilogy, Protective Shadows, is currently in progress.
