About

Silas Caste is the pen name of Jason Witt.

He began writing under a pen name for personal safety reasons while raising young children. His work draws from lived experience and explores themes of protection, justice, and the long aftermath of violence.

His first novel, Interrupted Dreams, is based on true events. The second book in the trilogy, Protective Shadows, is currently in progress.
